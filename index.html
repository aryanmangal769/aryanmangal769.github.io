Aryan Mangal

Hello! I am a Machine Learning Engineer at Woven by Toyota in Palo Alto. I currently work on foundation models for driving behavior, with a focus on pretraining and large-scale training and inference optimization. I recently completed my MS in Robotics at The Robotics Institute, Carnegie Mellon University (GPA 4.08/4), advised by Prof. Katia Sycara. Previously, I worked on human behavior anticipation for effective human–robot collaboration.

I received my B.Tech in Electronics and Communication from Delhi Technological University (GPA 9.5/10). As an undergrad I led the Unmanned Ground Vehicle team at DTU and was a 2022 CMU Robotics Institute Summer Scholar with Prof. Sebastian Scherer and Prof. Chen Wang.
